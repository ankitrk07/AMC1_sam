AMC 1 is not a knowledge test.
That's why good doctors fail it.

You already know medicine. You have treated patients that most Australian interns have only read about. That is not the problem.

The problem is that 150 questions in 210 minutes will ask you to think inside a system you have never worked in — Australian guidelines, Australian referral pathways, Australian thresholds for investigation. The exam does not reward the most knowledgeable doctor. It rewards the doctor who can reason the way an Australian clinician reasons, at ninety seconds per question, for three and a half hours.

That skill is learnable. It just has to be taught by someone who has learnt it.
