How the AMC 1 Program Works

The program runs as a live, structured cohort. You attend 2–3 interactive classes a week on Google Meet, and every session is uploaded to your recorded library within 24 hours — so hospital shifts or time zones never cost you a lecture.

Each module closes with a topic-wise mini mock, and the full course builds up to advanced full-length mock exams plus a free 2-week crash course right before your real AMC attempt.
