FAQ

Questions Doctors Ask Us.

How much time do I need each week?

Most students study around 12–15 hours a week, including live classes. We schedule the course so doctors working clinical shifts can attend or catch the same-day recording.

I’m working full-time in a hospital. Can I keep up?

Yes. The programme is designed for doctors balancing hospital work, night shifts, and exam prep. Every class is recorded and uploaded the same day, and the mock schedule can be planned around your roster.

What if I’ve already failed once?

Then we start by identifying the exact reason the first attempt did not work — timing, weak areas, or a mismatch with Australian-style reasoning. We do not restart you from zero; we build a targeted plan around what actually went wrong.

How is DRG different from other AMC courses?

Every mentor here has passed the AMC and practises as a doctor in Australia. We do not hire professional tutors to teach a system they have never worked in.

What is your actual pass rate, and how do you calculate it?

We report the pass rate transparently as a percentage of students from completed batches who sat the exam and passed. We publish the denominator because a pass rate without context is not meaningful.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Flexible instalment options are available at no extra cost, so you can begin without delaying your preparation.

What happens on the call?

Twenty minutes, one-to-one with a councellor. We map your prep timeline, explain the programme and answer everything on eligibility, the exam process and fees. There is no pressure to enrol.

Do you help after AMC 1?

Yes. We support students beyond AMC 1 with guidance on the next step of the Australian pathway, including AMC 2 preparation and study planning where relevant.
